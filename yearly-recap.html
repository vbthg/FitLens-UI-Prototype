ABOUT THIS RECAP

A year, illustrated.

This prototype uses a fictional 2025 activity history. Numbers, places and journal entries demonstrate the design; they are not your personal records.

71 hours = 42 walking + 18 strength + 7 running + 4 cycling. The 512 km covers recorded walks, runs and rides, without adding daily steps again.

Energy is estimated active energy. Elevation is cumulative ascent. Comparisons use approximate reference weights: 6,000 kg per bus, 180 kg per bear; a TV episode is 30 minutes and a building is 30 metres tall.

The map is illustrative. A real recap would need recorded route history and your permission to show identifiable places. Sharing this poster includes no location or health notes.
