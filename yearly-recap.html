ABOUT THIS RECAP

Your training year, illustrated.

This preview uses a fictional 2025 training history: 72 workouts, 54 hours, 864 working sets and 84,200 kg of lifting volume. It does not read your personal records yet.

Monthly workout counts add to 72. A week is highlighted when it includes a completed workout. Exercise appearances can overlap within a session.

The Goblet Squat comparison uses two illustrative sets of the same exercise and the same load convention: one dumbbell. It is not a maximum-strength test.

Volume = logged weight × completed reps. Playful comparisons use approximate weights: 6,000 kg per bus and 180 kg per bear. Each TV episode is 30 minutes.

The return after 17 days describes a gap between recorded workouts; it does not assume why you took a break. Rest is part of training.
