# Yearly recap illustrations

User-provided SVGs from `Downloads/recaps-year/svg`, accepted with their traced texture intact. Only root viewport dimensions and padding were normalized; original paths and colors are preserved. Source files in Downloads were not modified.

Recap comparisons are illustrative: 180 kg per bear, 6,000 kg per bus, and 30 minutes per TV episode. These are scale references, not claims about a specific animal, vehicle or programme.
